## Assembly and measured reading

1. The opening starts at exactly zero construction until an intentional scroll input. Restored scroll positions, initial layout and image/font loading must not start the build. The controller recognizes wheel, touch, keyboard and scrollbar intent. Returning to the page start before completion resets construction and motion to the empty centered opening; after completion the latch still preserves the assembled scene. Final real-browser coverage remains with the integrator.
2. Scroll builds About me (`stack`), then its readable content, then HorAgora/content, then ASC/content, and finally the orange 3D question mark. The assembly scene stays in place while the visitor progresses. Each build/replay eases its group from −135° to front-facing 0°, with distance multiplier 1.18→1 and elevation allowance 0.153→0. `buildMotion` uses smoothstep on that model’s build range; these are presentation transforms, not extra page-scroll bands.
3. `public/city-story.js` maps actual scroll pixels to story bands. Card overflow is measured at the current desktop/compact width; each reading segment reserves that overflow plus 400px. Reading offset begins after its initial allowance and reaches the full measured overflow before the next build. Resize and expanded project details trigger measurement again. Total track height is `ceil(max(board height, viewport height − measured header height) + sum(chapter lengths))`. Keeping the viewport allowance when the board is height-capped leaves enough scroll distance to reach the last scene; the fractions remain the internal model mapping.
4. Build spans are viewport-dependent: castle `max(850px, 1.2 × viewport height)`, each project `max(600px, .9 × height)`, question mark `max(550px, .8 × height)`. `STORY_BANDS` remains an internal model mapping; its fractions are not fixed percentages of the page's scroll distance. There is no timed wait during the guided build.
5. The opening shows only its centered intro cue: a 26px SVG in a 44px button, visually hidden accessible text and a 1.6-second bounce outside reduced motion. Clicking begins the build scroll. Once `construction-started`, a shared upper toolbar shows icon + short visible **Continuar / Pular** labels (localized in EN/ES), with minimum 44px targets; desktop top45px aligns it with the card header. **Continue** scrolls by `min(600px, .7 × viewport height)`; **Skip** completes instantly and has the “Montagem rápida” tooltip. The toolbar is hidden before construction and after completion. Selection/manual exploration unlock after completion or skip; reading/fallback never depends on finishing 3D.
6. Completion latches, collapses the assembly track and reveals the shared reading content. Scrolling back does not dismantle the models. Full **Restart assembly** is a separate explicit control that returns to the empty start.

These fades do not add a dependency or a scroll wait.

There is no separate cube/3D mode to unlock rotation, and rotation does not hide the content card. A separate local assembly replay lasts 4.2 seconds for the selected landmark; the other completed buildings stay assembled. This does not reset the completed story or replace full Restart assembly. Figures remain in their static poses while moving with their construction group.

Each landmark starts automatic 360° Y rotation at **12°/second (30 seconds per turn)** as soon as its own build finishes, including the guided reading pause before the full tour ends. The resident retains a static pose within the rotating group. Once manual exploration is unlocked, a quick flick coasts with a 20rad/s velocity cap, exponential damping1.6 and a 2.6-second coast limit (about two turns at the cap). Slow gestures hold for 0.7 seconds; both paths then return to front along the shortest angle with exponential easing rate4 before resuming automatic rotation. A tap during coasting interrupts it for the soft return. First manual input cancels a local replay without discarding the manual pose. Dialog/hidden/offscreen/reduced states pause motion; replay uses the build transform instead of idle rotation. `public/city-motion.js` holds pure build/coast math; controller state is in `public/city.js`. Any active visible landmark can keep the frame loop running; no benchmark, CPU/GPU saving or final visual acceptance is inferred.

A decorative PT/en/es hint appears at the bottom of the card only while more content remains below and disappears at the end. It is an absolutely positioned sibling, outside card flow, with no pointer interaction and `aria-hidden`; it does not change the measured card height. `initViewerOverflow` coalesces scroll/load/disclosure/resize/mutation updates into a pending animation frame, with observer/listener cleanup rather than a continuous measurement loop.
